# LocalLoop

LocalLoop is a neighborhood marketplace for discovering and offering items in Dhaka, with prices in Bangladeshi taka.

## Language

**Listing**: An offer by a seller describing an item, its asking price, condition, photos, and neighborhood.
_Avoid_: Product, advertisement

**Seller**: The person who owns a listing and offers its item.

**Buyer**: A person who contacts a seller about a listing; this does not imply a completed purchase.

**Neighborhood**: The approximate Dhaka area associated with a profile or listing, rather than an exact address.
_Avoid_: Exact location

**Saved listing**: A listing a visitor or user has bookmarked for later.
_Avoid_: Cart, order

**Listing draft**: An unfinished listing that has not been published.

**Conversation**: A private text exchange between a buyer and seller about a listing.

**Demo mode**: A fictional marketplace experience whose changes belong only to the visitor trying it.

**Live mode**: The shared marketplace sandbox, with public browsing and approved test accounts for participation.

**Approved test account**: An account authorized to publish listings and participate in messaging during the portfolio sandbox release. Unapproved accounts may still edit their own profiles.

**Simulated reply**: A clearly labeled fictional response in a demo conversation.

**Sold listing**: A listing its seller has marked as no longer available because the item was sold; LocalLoop does not process payment or verify the sale.

**Removed listing**: A deleted offer that is no longer discoverable or open to new conversations. Existing participants retain a minimal reference alongside read-only conversation history.

**Free listing**: A listing with a zero-taka asking price.

**Condition**: The seller's description of an item's physical state, chosen from Like new, Good, or Fair.
